# Code of Conduct

TraceForge is a security-engineering project. Contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, respectful and
constructive.

**Expected:** precise, evidence-based feedback; credit for other people's work; patience with newcomers; responsible
disclosure of vulnerabilities through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

**Not acceptable:** harassment or discrimination of any kind, personal attacks, publishing other people's private
information, and posting real credentials, personal data or production logs in issues or pull requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation for repeated
violations. Report concerns through a private security advisory or by contacting the maintainer listed on the profile.
